# Privacy Policy  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-01

This Privacy Policy explains how **Miami Creators** we collects, uses, and protects personal information from individuals who use our platform, including **Creators** and **Brands**.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information voluntarily provided by users in two primary contexts: **Creators** and **Brands**.

### 1.1 From Creators
When a Creator registers or submits a form on the Platform, we may collect:

- Full name and photograph
- Age of the creator
- Content niche or category
- Social media links (TikTok, Instagram, etc.)
- Portfolio links or video samples
- Self-written biography or description
- E-mail address

### 1.2 From Brands
When a Brand registers or purchases access to the Creator Catalog, we may collect:

- Business name
- Representative name and contact details (e-mail, phone)
- Company website or social media links
- Campaign details or creative requirements (optional)

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Data
When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

- IP address and device information
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited, timestamps, and referring URLs
- General geographic location (city, state, country)

This data is used only for analytics, security, and platform optimization.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business purposes, including to:

- Operate and maintain the Platform
- Create and manage user accounts
- Display Creator profiles in the private catalog accessible only to verified, paying Brands
- Communicate with users about campaigns, opportunities, or updates
- Verify identities and ensure accuracy of submissions
- Provide customer support
- Improve services and user experience
- Enforce our Terms and comply with legal requirements

We **do not sell, rent, or trade user data** to third parties.

---

## 3. E-mail Communications and Opt-Out

By providing your e-mail address, you consent to receive:

- Notifications about collaboration opportunities
- Platform updates or community news
- Account-related transactional messages

You can opt out at any time by clicking **“unsubscribe”** in our emails or contacting:  
**support@miamicreator.co**

Even after opting out, we may still send legal or account-related notices.

---

## 4. Data Visibility and Sharing

### 4.1 Creator Catalog
Creator profiles are **visible only** to verified Brands with purchased access.  
The public cannot view Creator data.  
Brands are prohibited from copying, reselling, or sharing Creator information.

### 4.2 Third-Party Service Providers
We may use third-party vendors (e.g., hosting, analytics, payment processors) to operate the Platform.  
These parties only access data needed to perform their services and are bound by confidentiality.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, government request, or to protect our legal rights or users.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law.

Generally:

- Data is retained while an account is active.
- Accounts inactive for over **24 months** may be archived or deleted.
- Users may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect personal information.

However, **no online platform can guarantee absolute security**, and use of the Platform is at your own risk.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is intended for users **18 and older**.  
We do not knowingly collect data from minors.  
If you believe we have collected such data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your Rights (Florida Digital Bill of Rights)

Under the **Florida Digital Bill of Rights (FDBR)**, you may:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Opt out of targeted advertising or data sharing
- Request a portable copy of your data

To exercise these rights, contact:  
**support@miamicreator.co**

We may require identity verification.
